#+TITLE: Evolving Music with LLMs: A Comparative Study
#+DATE: 2026-02-17

* The Experiment

We give four frontier LLMs the same seed -- a piece of music written in
Strudel (a live-coding language for music) -- and ask them to repeatedly
"evolve" it. Each model runs independently for 30 steps, receiving its
own previous output each time. The prompt is minimal:

#+begin_quote
Evolve this according to your preferences
#+end_quote

The system prompt is three lines: return only code, no markdown fences,
keep it under 500 lines. No style guidance, no music theory, no
examples. We want to see what each model's aesthetic instincts produce.

** Models

- Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Anthropic API, direct)
- GPT-4.1 (OpenRouter)
- Gemini 2.5 Pro (OpenRouter)
- Grok 4.1 Fast (OpenRouter)

** Seeds

- Bach BWV 1087 Canon 3 (two-voice canon with bass)
- Bach BWV 1087 Canon 9 (two-voice canon with bass)
- Twinkle Twinkle Little Star (simple melody, triangle wave)

* Observations

** The Grok Collapse

Grok has a distinctive failure mode: it periodically deletes everything.
A piece will grow normally over several steps (1162 -> 1693 -> 1961
chars), then suddenly collapse to near-zero (40 chars -- just a
comment). This happened consistently across multiple seeds and runs.

Example from Bach Canon 3, step 10:
#+begin_src js
// BWV 1087 Canon 4 Hyper-Evolved v6

//
#+end_src

That's the entire output. The previous step was nearly 2000 characters
of working music.

Same pattern in Canon 9: steps 0-3 grew normally, step 4 collapsed to 5
lines (just a voice definition, no notes).

This appears to be a Grok-specific behavior -- the other models never
exhibited it. We added a guard: if the output is less than 20% of the
input length, we reject it and keep the previous code.

** The Complexity Explosion

All models share a tendency to make the music increasingly complex with
each step. What starts as a simple melody quickly becomes a dense wall
of layered tracks, effects chains, and modulations. By step 15-20, a
simple Twinkle Twinkle has become a 10-track orchestral piece with FM
synthesis, ring modulation, shabda speech samples, and euclidean
rhythms.

Claude is the worst offender -- producing 15-20k character outputs per
step (compared to GPT's 5-10k and Grok's 2-5k). More isn't better:
the resulting music is often an overwhelming wash of sound where no
individual voice is discernible.

This is the "more is more" instinct that all LLMs seem to share when
given creative freedom. Left unchecked, they treat "evolve" as "add
more stuff" rather than "refine what's there." We added a prompt
instruction to prefer clarity over complexity.
