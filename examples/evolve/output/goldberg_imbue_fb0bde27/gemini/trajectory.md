# Gemini Trajectory - Run 1 (goldberg_imbue_fb0bde27)

**Step 0** starts as a minimal single-track piece: the Goldberg ground bass (G3-Gb3-E3-D3-B2-C3-D3-G2) on triangle at 72 BPM, gain 0.5, with light reverb. 1 track, no drums, no speech samples, no effects chain to speak of.

**By step 10**, the tempo has risen to 80 BPM and the piece has expanded to 6 tracks. The ground bass is still present but quieter (gain 0.2) on pulse with perlin-modulated waveshaping and a lowpass sweep. A G minor scale run on triangle (gain 0.18) with phaser and delay has been added, plus a four-on-the-floor kick at gain 0.9 with bitcrush and drive -- by far the loudest element. Hi-hats sit at 0.3 with a high-pass filter. A supersaw pad plays Gm7/Cmaj7 chords very slowly (slow 16) at gain 0.2 with heavy reverb (0.8). Speech samples ("evolve", "future", "personality") appear chopped and distorted at gain 0.4. The character has shifted from baroque simplicity to an electronic/IDM hybrid with drums dominating.

**Step 20** pushes the tempo to 110 BPM. Still 6 tracks. The bass has moved to sawtooth with bitcrush(5) and Euclidean rhythms, now transposed down 2 semitones (gain 0.15). The scale run is now on supersaw at fast(8), very quiet at gain 0.1. Drums remain prominent: kick at gain 0.6 with crush(5), compressor, and drive(0.7). Hi-hats have acquired vowel filtering and bandpass modulation at gain 0.15. The chord pad uses Ab maj7/Eb m7 voicings with tremolo sync and phaser. A Japanese speech sample ("mirai") joins the existing speech bank, now with Euclidean rhythms and randomized speed including negative values (reverse playback). The piece has become denser and more aggressive, with more modulation on every parameter.

**Step 30** reaches 120 BPM with 6 tracks. The harmonic language has drifted far from G major -- the bass plays Eb2/Gb2/Db3/Bb2 on pulse with FM synthesis at gain 0.1. Piano appears for the first time playing scale-degree patterns in Gb major with jux panning (gain 0.1). Drums have calmed somewhat: kick at gain 0.4 with crush(6), mid-toms and claps at gain 0.18. The supersaw pad plays Eb m11 / Db M9 chords at gain 0.11 with heavy resonant filter envelope. Spanish speech ("futuro") joins the multilingual sample set. Overall, Gemini's trajectory in Run 1 goes from a bare ground bass to a 6-track electronic piece that accelerates steadily (72->80->110->120 BPM), adds drums and speech early and keeps them throughout, and progressively moves away from the original G major tonality toward flat keys (Ab, Eb, Gb, Db). Gain levels trend downward on melodic elements while drums stay loud. The piece accumulates effects (bitcrush, FM, vowel filters, Euclidean rhythms) without ever removing them.
