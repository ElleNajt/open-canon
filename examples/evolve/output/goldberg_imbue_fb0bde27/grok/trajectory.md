# Grok Trajectory - Run 1 (goldberg_imbue_fb0bde27)

Grok starts from the bare Goldberg ground bass: a single triangle voice at 72 BPM playing the 8-note descending line, gain 0.5. By step 10 it has jumped to 98 BPM with 6 tracks: piano with jux(rev), a supersaw sub-bass with FM, a euclidean drum kit (euclid(7,16) kick, euclid(6,16) snare, hh*8), a sawtooth melody with perlin-controlled filter, and two layers of chopped shabda speech samples ("goldberg_variations", "bach") with vowel filtering and ring modulation. Gains range from 0.17 to 0.7. The approach is already maximalist at step 10 -- there is no gradual build from the seed material.

From step 10 to 40, the trajectory is a steady escalation in tempo, density, and effect saturation. Tempo rises linearly: 98, 128, 136, 152 BPM. The drum euclidean densities increase from euclid(7,16) to euclid(13,16) for kicks and euclid(11,16) for snares by step 30, holding there through step 40. Gain levels climb across the board -- kick gains go from 0.7 to 0.98, snare from 0.4 to 0.92. The synth palette stays consistent (supersaw, pulse, sawtooth) but accumulates more effects: step 20 adds compressor and crush(5), step 30 adds ring modulation and detune on the bass, step 40 introduces echo(5,0.1,0.4) and perlin-modulated swing. A "sine" pad track appears at step 30 with tremolo, adding a 6th melodic voice.

By step 40, the piece is heavily saturated. Nearly every parameter is modulated by perlin or sine LFOs, including the `.slow()` values themselves (e.g., `.slow(perlin.range(1.8,6.2))`), making the rhythmic structure unstable. The shabda speech samples are tripled and chopped into 32 pieces with perlin-controlled speed. A piano track reappears at step 40 after being absent since step 10. The overall arc is: simple seed -> immediate 6-track explosion -> relentless gain/tempo/density escalation with no pullback or dynamic contrast. The piece never breathes.
