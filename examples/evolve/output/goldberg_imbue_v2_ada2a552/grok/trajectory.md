# Grok Trajectory - Run 2 (goldberg_imbue_v2_ada2a552)

Grok starts from the same Goldberg ground bass seed (72 BPM, single triangle voice, gain 0.5). By step 10 it has already built out to 6 tracks at 116 BPM, using different shabda speech words than Run 1: "ghosts_in_the_machine" and "evolve" instead of "goldberg_variations" and "bach". The musical material shifts away from the Goldberg theme -- the lead melody is in Eb with a saw synth, and there is a scale-degree pattern in G dorian. Drums use euclid(4,16) for kicks and euclid(12,16) for hi-hats, with crush(9). Gains are moderate (0.24-0.7).

The tempo escalation is steeper than Run 1: 72, 116, 148, 178 BPM across 4 steps (vs. Run 1's 72, 98, 128, 136, 152 across 5 steps). By step 20 (148 BPM), the scale has shifted to Eb phrygian, distortion is at 0.42 on the bass, ring modulation appears on both the bass and supersaw pad, and the drums gain shape (waveshaping) and delay effects. Speech samples get more prominent (gain 0.55, up from 0.24). Step 30 hits 178 BPM -- the fastest tempo across all three runs -- with Eb minor scale, drums crushed with perlin.range(4,12), and piano gain at 0.7 with heavy FM and detune up to +68 cents. The speech samples are heavily multiplied (evolve*4, evolve*5, ghosts_in_the_machine*2) and chopped with sine-modulated chop counts.

Compared to Run 1, Run 2 diverges more from the Goldberg source material (moving to Eb-centered harmony early), escalates tempo faster, and reaches a higher peak BPM. Both runs share the same structural pattern: immediate jump from 1 track to 6, then monotonic increase in tempo and effect density with no dynamic variation or subtraction. Run 2 is the shortest at only 4 steps (30 total iterations vs. Run 1's 40).
