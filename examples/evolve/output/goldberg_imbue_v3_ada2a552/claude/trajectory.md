## Overall trajectory

The run starts from a single bare ground bass line (the Goldberg aria bass, `g3 fs3 e3 d3 b2 c3 d3 g2`) on triangle at a comfortable 0.5 gain. By step 10, it has already expanded to 6 tracks covering 5 octaves (g1 through d6), but with total gain summing to only about 0.83. The build uses just three synth types throughout the entire run: triangle, sine, and sawtooth. Tempo never changes from 72 BPM.

From step 10 onward, the evolution is almost entirely subtractive and textural rather than additive. The track count stays fixed at 6 for all remaining steps. Gains fall steadily from a sum of ~0.83 at step 10 to ~0.50 by step 40, then stabilize. Attack and release envelopes stretch dramatically -- by step 20, several voices have attacks of 2.5-4 seconds and releases of 14-16 seconds, making the piece increasingly drone-like. Randomness accumulates: `degradeBy` appears on 2 of 6 voices by step 30, and `sometimes`/`often` transpositions (fifths and octaves) appear on 2 more voices, making the harmonic content increasingly unpredictable per cycle.

The most interesting structural change is in the sub-bass voice: it grows from a 4-note pedal tone pattern at step 10 to a 20-note wandering line by step 60, cycling over `.slow(8)` -- effectively a slow-moving counter-melody in the lowest register. By the end, the piece is quiet, spacious, and harmonically unstable, with most notes arriving softly through long fades and many being randomly dropped. The ground bass is still there but heavily filtered and shaped. It reads as a gradual dissolution of the original theme into an ambient texture.
