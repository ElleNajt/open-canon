# Grok Trajectory - Run 3 (goldberg_imbue_v3_ada2a552)

Grok starts from the identical Goldberg ground bass seed (72 BPM, triangle, gain 0.5). By step 10 it reaches 98 BPM with 6 tracks -- the same tempo as Run 1's step 10 but with a different character. The shabda speech samples are the most varied of any run: "groove", "pulse", "evolve", "grok", "mutate", "change" (6 words vs. 2-3 in the other runs). The melodic material stays closer to the Goldberg bass line (Bb-centered, descending motion in the bass). Gains at step 10 are notably lower than the other runs (0.2-0.48), with the drums at gain 0.42 for kicks and 0.2 for hi-hats. A palindrome() is used on the triangle melody. The feel is more restrained than the other runs at the same point.

Tempo escalation follows the same pattern as Run 2: 72, 98, 148, 178 BPM. The jump from 98 to 148 between steps 10 and 20 is the largest single tempo increase across all runs (+50 BPM). At step 20, two more speech words are added ("strudel", "livecode" -- 8 total), vowel filtering appears on the speech track, and drums add delay with delayfeedback(0.4). The synth palette keeps piano and triangle through step 20 (unique to this run -- the other runs drop these early for heavier synths). By step 30, piano and triangle are gone, replaced by sawtooth and square. Gains peak at 0.74-0.85 for the synths, 0.85 for kicks. A compressor appears on the drum bus. The speech track gains reach perlin.range(0.7,1.1), meaning they can exceed unity gain.

Run 3's distinguishing features: it uses the most shabda words (8 by step 20), retains softer timbres (piano, triangle) longer than the other runs, and introduces lpq (filter resonance) modulation at step 30, which neither other run uses. Like the other runs, it follows a strictly additive trajectory with no moments of reduction or contrast. All three Grok runs share this fundamental pattern: seed -> immediate full instrumentation -> monotonic escalation in tempo, gain, effect count, and modulation depth.
